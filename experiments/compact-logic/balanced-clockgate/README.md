# Full gating with a balanced primary-clock domain

This isolated `compact_23b` experiment retains the current full-width RTL at source SHA256 `5c7a4656036b7c30ca5badb615a74ebcb889459cdab76033536a1aa6e16ebe0b`. It changes physical clock implementation only. The fixed native clock-gating pass still ties every TE low. A further native `icgtp_1` with E tied high and TE tied low supplies the 317 flip-flops otherwise clocked directly by the primary clock. The other 281 flip-flops remain behind their 15 functional ICGs. Both domains can then receive comparable clock-tree insertion delay.

The pinned PDK model forms `D = E | TE`, latches that while CLK is low, and produces `Q = CLK & latched_D`. With native tie-high E and tie-low TE, the gate follows CLK after an initial low phase. `check_always_icg.py` tests the actual unmodified PDK model through 128 cycles / 256 edges after low initialization. This is a scoped functional test, not a new transistor-level or whole-design formal proof. The unchanged external-pin regression passes all 44 cases on the exact newly synthesized netlist. The additional post-global-route structural test follows all 598 FF clock nets through their buffers/inverters to the 16 ICGs; exactly 317 reach the always-enabled gate. Every TE is a native tie-low, and the always-enabled E is a native tie-high.

Synthesis area is 78,851.584 µm². Post-CTS timing repair inserts 17 hold buffers and legalizes, compared with 172 buffers and failed placement in the previous full-gating experiment. Standard-cell area including physical support is 89,327.1 µm² after CTS repair and 89,526.8 µm² after global-route repair. Global routing and all required post-global-route repair stages pass; one antenna violation was repaired to zero. This package does not claim detailed-route completion or final signoff.

The exact pinned nominal-TT post-global-route STA replay covers 598 propagated FF clocks, both CLK/Q edges at all 16 gates, all 1,196 FF pulse checks, and 16 targeted ICG pulse checks. The 15 dynamic gates have explicit setup/hold enable checks. The always-enabled gate has no timed enable path because its E is tied high; the summary verifies that connection rather than pretending a dynamic check exists. Worst reported data hold/setup slacks are +0.415047/+511.233459 ns; dynamic ICG enable setup/hold margins are +611.490662/+3.838511 ns; minimum pulse margin is +311.613739 ns. No clock-gating or pulse violations occur. This is one placement/global-route parasitic corner, not routed nine-corner signoff.

The first `compact_23` attempt failed before rewiring at an unsupported pyosys `Module.cells()` iterator. The corrected `compact_23b` uses the supported `selected_cells()` iterator; original failed source and logs remain in ignored build evidence. The original prelaunch receipt also included `tools/run_compact_trial.py`, which changed concurrently. That helper was never invoked by this trial's recorded direct command; the result receipt explicitly discloses it. All executed synthesis/flow inputs, RTL, constraints, and macro bytes were unchanged during the trial. Audit inputs were independently hashed before and after and match.

Raw runs live at `physical/runs/compact_23b`; command receipts identify exact stages and inputs. `manifest.json` hashes every retained package file. Native model files are external pinned PDK dependencies with exact hashes in the test receipts. The root release remains the inherited verified 2×2 release and is not qualified by this partial 1×2 experiment.
